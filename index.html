Frequently asked questions

Details about the mathematical unblending technique, privacy, and media compatibility.

How does the blend reversal work?

The mark is a semi-transparent layer blended over the picture. The tool knows its shape and opacity, so it solves the blend equation for the original pixel instead of painting over it.

Original = (Watermarked − α × Logo) ÷ (1 − α)

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. By default everything runs in your browser and nothing leaves your device. If you choose a local engine, files go only to the address you set.

Can I process many images and videos at once?

Yes. Use Batch to add files, folders or a ZIP. You get one ZIP of cleaned files back.

Does this remove invisible watermarks?

No. It reverses only the visible sparkle mark. Any invisible provenance data stays in the pixels.

What can go wrong?

Heavily compressed or resized files may not restore perfectly because the pixel values no longer match the known blend. Use the fine tuning controls and compare to check the result.
